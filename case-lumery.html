Lumery

Shaping an AI wearable as one connected physical, digital and visual product.

RoleProduct / Visual Designer
PeriodDec 2025—May 2026
TeamCEO + 2 designers

Product question

How can AI live on the face and still feel like a personal object, not a gadget?

One product across hardware, software and brand.

Lumery began as an early-stage smart-glasses product. Working directly with the CEO and one other designer, I helped develop the product direction, visual language, companion experience and interaction logic as one coherent system.

Physical product

Designed around
what has to live inside.

The frame had to balance a familiar eyewear silhouette with the real constraints of an AI device.

Product principles

Technology is present, but the object remains quiet, wearable and understandable.

  • Keep the front silhouette close to everyday eyewearWearability
  • Use the temples to organize the technical architectureIntegration
  • Turn status and control into restrained light cuesFeedback
  • Extend the same geometry into charging and packagingEcosystem

Digital product

The interface continues
what the glasses start.

The companion product organizes captured moments, system states and personal context without falling back on a generic dashboard.

Visions

Personal knowledge system

Save without organizing.
Find without remembering.

MyMind unifies visual search, photos, voice notes, OCR and assistant answers into one card model. Categories describe what something is; collections describe why it matters to the user.
  1. CaptureGlasses, phone, voice or assistant answer
  2. Create a MindCardMedia + OCR / ASR + entities + provenance
  3. Classify by intentShopping, Research, Food, Work and more
  4. Resolve confidenceHigh → category · uncertain → lightweight Intake
  5. RetrieveSemantic search, filters, related cards and collections
  6. ResurfaceUseful knowledge returns by context, time or behavior
System layer

Categories

Automatic structure based on the nature and intent of a capture.

User layer

Collections

Cross-category stacks for projects, plans, comparison and inspiration.

Quality layer

Intake

A short review queue only when classification confidence is insufficient.

Living travel map

Raw moments become
a story worth returning to.

MyWorld converts hands-free captures, movement and location context into trips, routes, moments and AI-built excursions. The map is the primary interface, not a conventional content feed.
  1. Collect signalsMedia, voice, time, location and motion state
  2. Build eventsCluster captures by time, place and movement
  3. Segment scenesArrival, walk, museum, café, sunset
  4. Form a tripRoute + places + moments + AI summary
  5. Generate excursionSelected scenes, narrative, timeline and highlights
  6. ReplayA cinematic travel memory instead of another feed

Opt-in social layer

Share what you saw.
Not a performance of yourself.

A Vision can only come from a real MyMind card, collection or MyWorld moment. Publishing is always explicit; exact locations stay hidden and every shared object can be saved back into someone else’s MyMind.
  1. Start from private contentMindCard, collection, moment or excursion
  2. Choose Share as VisionNo empty posts or content created only for reach
  3. Review privacyPreview, coarse location and sensitive-content checks
  4. Publish a Vision CardVisual, short context, category and region
  5. React quietlyFeel-based reactions without public competition
  6. Save and reuseA Vision returns to MyMind as useful knowledge

Decision

Automation with an escape hatch

The system organizes automatically, but exposes confidence, Intake and editing whenever certainty is low.

Decision

One object model across sources

Camera, voice, OCR and assistant output become consistent cards instead of separate content silos.

Decision

Search as human memory

Semantic retrieval uses text, OCR, transcripts, entities and context — not exact filenames or keywords.

Decision

Privacy is part of the structure

Private-by-default data, explicit sharing and coarse location are product rules, not settings added later.

Visual language

A monochrome base gives the product calm. Light, dots and digital blocks make it feel alive.

A system able to move between object and interface.

The identity combines restrained typography, technical line work and a modular blue signal. The same ingredients extend into product UI, web communication, 3D scenes and motion studies.
